8.1 · BEATING A BETTER TEAM PAYS MORE

How the rating works

Everyone starts at 1500. The gap between the two sides decides what the match is worth, so beating a stacked crew is worth three times beating a weak one. Nobody climbs by farming easy opponents.

Who you playedWinLose
Evenly matched+16−16
A better team+24−8
A worse team+8−24

The first 10 matches are placement games — your rating moves faster and you stay off the public board until they are done.

8.3 · WHAT IT DOES

Features

Player + team

Two ladders that never mix, and a separate board per game mode. A specialist at one mode never quietly carries the other.

MVP voting

Buttons on every result. The players in that match vote, the winner earns a rating bonus, and you can take MVP off a loss.

Rank roles

A coloured Discord role per tier, assigned and swapped automatically as ratings move.

Undo that works

A wrong result is cancelled with one command. Every rating it touched goes back exactly, including matches played afterwards.

Predictions

Optional play-money betting on upcoming matches, settled automatically. It can never move anyone’s actual rating.

Public boards

Top players and crews published to the web, refreshed every minute, alongside the rest of the network.
